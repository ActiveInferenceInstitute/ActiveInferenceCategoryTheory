# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving the Active Inference & Category Theory curriculum!

This repository is a collaborative, community-maintained curriculum. The syllabus itself invites suggestions and improvements — see the Notes section of [IntroAdventureSyllabus.md](IntroAdventureSyllabus.md).

## How to contribute

- **Open an issue** for questions, suggestions, or corrections — e.g. a broken link, a typo, or a resource that should be added.
- **Open a pull request** with concrete changes to [IntroAdventureSyllabus.md](IntroAdventureSyllabus.md) or this documentation. Keep edits faithful to their sources: verify links and citations before changing them.
- **Discuss** on the Active Inference Institute [Discord](https://discord.gg/BEpgX3z7) (Category Theory channel) or [X / Twitter](https://twitter.com/InferenceActive).

## Guidelines

- Every link and citation should point to a real, accessible resource. If you cannot verify a link, say so in the pull request description.
- Preserve the friendly, "adventure mode" voice of the curriculum.
- Prefer plain Markdown; keep lines readable and avoid trailing whitespace.
